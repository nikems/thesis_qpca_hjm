E2.1  REALISING U = e^{i t rho} AND ITS COST
==============================================================================
   martin_2x2: rho = 0.5000 I + 0.3288 X + 0.1407 Z   (c_P = Tr(rho P)/2: only the ENTRIES of rho are needed)
      closed form e^(it rho) = e^(it/2)[cos(t r) I + i sin(t r) (n.sigma)], r = 0.3576: max|closed - expm| = ~0 (< 10^-12)  (single-qubit gate, NO Trotter error)
      controlled-U^(2^k), k=0..7, transpiled (rz,sx,x,cx; opt 3): CX = [2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2]  depth = [11, 11, 11, 11, 11, 11, 11, 11]
   ecb_2x2: rho = 0.5000 I + 0.3693 X + -0.1688 Z   (c_P = Tr(rho P)/2: only the ENTRIES of rho are needed)
      closed form e^(it rho) = e^(it/2)[cos(t r) I + i sin(t r) (n.sigma)], r = 0.4061: max|closed - expm| = ~0 (< 10^-12)  (single-qubit gate, NO Trotter error)
      controlled-U^(2^k), k=0..7, transpiled (rz,sx,x,cx; opt 3): CX = [2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2]  depth = [11, 11, 11, 11, 11, 11, 11, 11]
   -> for one qubit every power U^(2^k) is again a single-qubit rotation: controlled cost <= 2 CX, independent of k.
      Realised by REPETITION of U, the same QPE would need 2^n - 1 applications of C-U (oracle calls) per shot.

   Density-matrix exponentiation (Lloyd et al. 2014): with K copies of rho and partial swaps,
   sigma -> Tr_1[ e^{i S d}(rho (x) sigma) e^{-i S d} ] = e^{i rho d} sigma e^{-i rho d} + O(d^2),  d = t/K
   martin_2x2: trace distance vs copies K: K=10: 0.21, K=30: 0.14, K=100: 0.060, K=300: 0.022, K=1000: 0.0069, K=3000: 0.0023, K=10000: 0.00070   slope -0.85 (theory -1);  K(eps=0.01, t=2pi) ~ 588
      copies of rho for a full n-bit QPE (eps = 0.01 per controlled power): n=2: 2940, n=4: 49980, n=6: 802,620, n=8: 12,844,860  (grows as 4^n)
   ecb_2x2: trace distance vs copies K: K=10: 0.20, K=30: 0.13, K=100: 0.051, K=300: 0.019, K=1000: 0.0057, K=3000: 0.0019, K=10000: 0.00058   slope -0.87 (theory -1);  K(eps=0.01, t=2pi) ~ 485
      copies of rho for a full n-bit QPE (eps = 0.01 per controlled power): n=2: 2425, n=4: 41225, n=6: 662,025, n=8: 10,594,825  (grows as 4^n)
   -> DME removes the need to know U, but costs ~4^n copies (and controlled partial swaps): it is the
      origin of the 'exponential speed-up' assumptions and of the practical limits discussed in E10.

E2.6  GATES OF THE PUBLIC 2x2 CODE  (github.com/amartinfer/QPCA, Qiskit 0.7.1)
==============================================================================
   cu3(theta,phi,lam) in Qiskit 0.7 = controlled[ e^{-i(phi+lam)/2} U3 ]  ('legacy');
   modern Qiskit CU3 = controlled[ U3 ]  ('modern'). Process fidelity F_pro = |Tr(A^dag B)|^2 / d^2.
   cu3 #1 (intended C-U^1):  F(legacy, U^1) = 1.000000   controlled: legacy 1.0000, modern 0.9490
   cu3 #2 (intended C-U^2):  F(legacy, U^2) = 1.000000   controlled: legacy 1.0000, modern 0.2545
   cu3 #3 (intended C-U^4):  F(legacy, U^4) = 0.487478   controlled: legacy 0.7210, modern 0.0285
   cu3 #3 equals Z U^4 Z = exp(2 pi i 4 Z rho Z) with F = 1.000000: the off-diagonal
   (covariance) entry of rho enters with the WRONG SIGN in the U^4 gate of QPCA_QPhE.py.
   With modern Qiskit semantics the controlled gates are not C-U (F = 0.95, 0.25, 0.03): running the public
   code unchanged in Qiskit >= 0.8 does NOT reproduce the paper (verified in E2.7).
